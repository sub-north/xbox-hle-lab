## What these rows establish

- **Package identity is not the gate.** The full identifier, the shortened 30-character identity, and
  an unpackaged answer all stop at `+0x1CE6E13`.
- **Staging is ruled out.** The archives the title probes — `appdata0.rpf`, `common.rpf`, `data.rpf`,
  `movies.rpf`, then script, shaders, textures and packs — are present and the stop does not move.
- **The `int3` guards something real.** Skipping it does not advance the title; execution reaches a
  write to address 0 inside `vcruntime140`.
- **At the stop the map is empty.** Its control bytes are all `0x80`, and both probed globals are null.
- **The pack is read, and its validity is not enough.** Zeroing `appdata0.rpf` moves the failure to
  `+0x2237320`; a structurally valid count-0 RPF9 is not accepted either and lands on the same fault.
- **`66e8e97` changes nothing here.** The stop is unchanged. The upstream report of a 45 s run and a
  first frame at 3.6 s is author-reported and does not reproduce against this title.

## On the record column

`archived log` means the row is backed by a saved guest log in our run archive; `run record` means the
run is recorded but no log survives, and the row states what was recorded and nothing more. The
archive is not published in this repository, so a reader can audit the labels but cannot open the
logs from here.

A blank pack is not a shortcut: the title rejects it at the same place as a zeroed one, so the question
is not whether the pack exists but what it carries.

The remaining lever is the contents of the per-title pack.
